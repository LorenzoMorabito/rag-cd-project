# Merge Notes With Existing Business Corpus

## Merge principle
Do not collapse business glossary entries and technical table profiles into a single object.

Use a layered merge:
- business glossary corpus = meaning / definition / business examples
- promo technical corpus = physical model / joins / keys / quality/readiness
- future quality layer = raw evidence / audit checks / duplicate diagnostics
