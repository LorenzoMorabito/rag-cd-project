# NPS Categories

## Canonical definition
NPS categories, resulting from the question: "Overall, how likely would you be to recommend this interaction to a colleague (0 = not at all likely, 10 = extremely likely) ?"

## Allowed values / key options
- Promoters (Ratings from 9 to 10)
- Passives (Ratings from 7 to 8)
- Detractors (Ratings from 0 to 6)

## Applicability
- Available for Detailing, Mailing & Meetings
