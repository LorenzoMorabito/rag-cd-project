# Traditional - Clinical Trials

## Canonical definition
Includes: Phase I & II (cost = zero as there is no promotion action during these phases) Phase III & IV: long term follow-up experimentation to check side effects. Observational Study, Post Marketing Surveillance (P.M.S.) and Expanded Access Program (E.A.P.)
