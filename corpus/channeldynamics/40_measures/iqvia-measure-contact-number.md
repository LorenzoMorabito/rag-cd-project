# Contact Number

## Canonical definition
Projected number of interactions (all channels)

## Allowed values / key options
- 1 Detailing contact = 1 Product Detail
- 1 Meeting contact = 1 participation to a meeting about the reported brand
- 1 Mailing contact = 1 Mailing received / read
- 1 Clinical Trial contact = 1 clinical trial reported during a fortnight
- 1 Sample contact = 1 Product Detail with Sample left for the brand
