# Non-Weighted Calls

## Canonical definition
Projected number of Calls to Physicians, without any weight applied - The non-weighted call metric can be used to analyze call activity of identifiable contract sales force companies that are presenting products from more than one manufacturer – or co-promoting companies that both present the same drug. The non-weighted value attributes a full call to each manufacturer of the products presented - To use only with « Manufacturer » or « Int. Corp
