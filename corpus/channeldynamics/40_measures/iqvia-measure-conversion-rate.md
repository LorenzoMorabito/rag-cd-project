# Conversion Rate

## Canonical definition
Corresponds to the % of converted calls within the total calls for every brand. "Converted Calls" are Frequent prescribers with intent to increase Rx or remain unchanged + Occasional & Never Prescribers with intent to increase Rx

## Applicability
- Available for Detailing, Mailing & Meetings
