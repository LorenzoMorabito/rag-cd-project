# Indication

## Canonical definition
ChannelDynamics data can be split by indication for designated areas: Anti-TNFs, Asthma-COPD, Oncology, NOACs, etc. When CD panellists record promotion for brands within these therapy areas, they are also asked to specify the indication for which the brand was promoted

## Applicability
- Available for Detailing, Mailing & Meeting channels only
