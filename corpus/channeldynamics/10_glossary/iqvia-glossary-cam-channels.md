# Cam_Channels

## Canonical definition
The communication channels covered are:

## Allowed values / key options
- CLINICAL TRIALS
- DTC = Direct to consumer (USA only for Rx brands, EU4 + UK for CHC brands)
- GP/FP DETAILING (Detailing to GPs or Family practitioners)
- JOURNAL ADVERTISING
- MAILING
- MEETINGS
- MS DETAILING (specific to Japan)
- NURSE DETAILING (Detailing to Diabetes or Practice Nurses, UK and USA only)
- OTHERS (Apps, Corporate Websites, Social Media, Merchandising)
- PHARMA DETAILING (Detailing to office pharmacists)
- SAMPLES
- SP DETAILING (Detailing to Specialists : Cardio, Rheumato, Gastro, etc.)
