# IntBrandname

## Canonical definition
Corresponds to the International Product Name in MIDAS: International Product is designed to match 2 out of 3 elements of product name, molecule and marketing corporation to provide a global brand name. The name of the international product is taken from the first launched product, unless a more widely used name is given to a product launched later
